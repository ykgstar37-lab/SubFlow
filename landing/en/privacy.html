Privacy Policy

Effective: August 26, 2026 · Last updated: August 26, 2026

SubFlow (the "Service") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws, and has adopted this policy to protect users' personal information.

1. Personal information we collect

A. On sign-up (required)

ItemPurpose
Email addressAccount identification, sign-in, sending notifications
PasswordAccount authentication (stored with one-way bcrypt hashing; the original is never kept)
Name (nickname)Shown inside the Service

B. Information you enter yourself

ItemPurpose
Subscription details (service name, price and currency, billing cycle and date, status, notes, etc.)Subscription management, analysis and reminders
Notification settings (timing, email/push, monthly budget)Providing notifications
Mobile push tokenSending app push notifications
Bug reports and feedback (what you write, email address, device, app version and browser information, the screen at the time of the report)Answering inquiries and finding the cause of errors

C. Information generated automatically

ItemPurpose
IP addressPreventing abuse; rate-limiting sign-in and sign-up requests
Service usage history (creating, changing and cancelling subscriptions; payment history)Timeline, statistics and price-increase detection

D. Information we do not collect

The Service never collects or stores real financial or payment information such as card or bank account numbers. A subscription's "amount" is a number you enter for your own tracking; actual payments happen at each subscription provider.

2. Why we use personal information

  • Identifying members and authenticating sign-in
  • Core features such as subscription management, spending analysis and budgets
  • Sending reminders (email and push) for billing dates, price increases, trial endings and budget overruns
  • Preventing abuse and keeping the Service stable

3. How long we keep it

  • Member information: kept until you delete your account, and destroyed without delay on request.
  • When you delete your account, your subscriptions, notification settings and usage history are deleted with it.
  • Where a law requires a separate retention period, we keep the data for that period.

4. Sharing with third parties and outsourcing

The Service never sells personal information or provides it to third parties. To run the Service, we outsource the following tasks.

ProcessorTaskData sent
Email delivery service (SMTP provider)Sending notification emailsRecipient email address, notification content
Expo (mobile push service)Sending app push notificationsPush token, notification content
OpenAISummarizing news articlesNews headlines and sources (no user personal information or subscription data is sent)

ℹ️ The news summary feature sends only public news headlines to the external AI. Personal data, such as which services you subscribe to, is never sent outside.

5. How we destroy personal information

  • We destroy data without delay once the retention period ends or its purpose is fulfilled.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

6. Your rights

You may at any time ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, ask us to stop processing it, or delete your account. You can edit or delete your account and subscriptions yourself in the Service's settings; other requests can be sent to the contact below.

7. How we keep it safe

  • One-way password hashing (bcrypt) — not even administrators can see the original
  • JWT-based authentication with token expiry and refresh
  • Rate limits on sign-in and sign-up (against brute-force attacks)
  • Secret keys and database credentials kept in separate environment variables
